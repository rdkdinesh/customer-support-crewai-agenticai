FAQ 1:
Question: What are your customer support hours?
Answer: Our customer support team is available Monday to Friday from 9 AM to 6 PM.

FAQ 2:
Question: How can I reset my password?
Answer: Go to the login page, click "Forgot Password", and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

FAQ 3:
Question: How can I cancel my order?
Answer: You can cancel an order from the Orders section of your account if the order has not yet been shipped.

FAQ 4:
Question: How can I contact customer support?
Answer: You can contact our customer support team through email or the customer support portal.

FAQ 5:
Question: What payment methods do you accept?
Answer: We accept major credit cards, debit cards, and supported online payment methods.

FAQ 6:
Question: How can I update my email address?
Answer: Log in to your account, open Profile Settings, update your email address, and save the changes.
